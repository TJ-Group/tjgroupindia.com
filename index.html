About TJ Group

Rooted in Family. Built for the Future.

TJ Group is a family-led enterprise founded by Durai Babu J. The identity of TJ Group is rooted in the family's heritage and carried forward through its commercial and residential properties.

The TJ Name

A name with family roots.

The initials TJ come from the names of two generations of the family's ancestors:

T — Tirumalai Naidu
J — Jayaraman Naidu

The name continues today as the identity of TJ Group, connecting the family's heritage with its present properties and future plans.
